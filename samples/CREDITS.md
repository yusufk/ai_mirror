# Sample clip credits

Test clips for the spatial audio panel. All are public domain or CC0, sourced
from Wikimedia Commons. Credit is given as a courtesy, it isn't required.

Each clip was trimmed to the section noted, loudness-normalised to -20 LUFS,
faded in/out, and encoded as 128 kbps stereo AAC.
